# PantryPal
Snap a photo of your fridge or pantry to automatically detect ingredients, discover delicious custom recipes tailored to your taste, and track your daily nutrition.

## Features
- <b>Fridge & Pantry Vision Scanner:</b> Upload or snap a photo of your shelves to identify ingredients and instantly craft step-by-step custom recipes.
- <b>Meal Calorie & Macro Estimator:</b> Take a photo of any cooked plate or snack to calculate calories, protein, carbs, fat, and log to your daily tracker.
- <b>Personalized Saved Recipes:</b> Save your favorite recipes, view step-by-step instructions, watch YouTube cooking tutorials, and log cooked meals.
